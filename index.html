MFA Setup

  1. Select SMS as your MFA method.
  2. Enter your phone number and confirm the correct country flag.
  3. Save your settings, then enter the verification code sent to your email.
  4. Log out of Back Office completely, log back in, and repeat the steps to add your bank account.

Bank Account Setup

  1. Log in to the EPOS Now Back Office.
  2. Select the nine-square menu, then Back Office.
  3. Go to Setup → Payments → Payment Setup.
  4. Select Pending Approval – Update Details and follow the prompts.
Open Payment Setup ↗

Card Reader Back Office Setup

  1. Go to Setup → Payments → Terminal Setup.
  2. Follow the on-screen setup prompts.
  3. Cloud Sync the till to apply the updated settings.

Integrating the Card Reader With the Till

  1. Open the three-line menu on the till.
  2. Go to Settings → Payments → EPOS Now Payments.
  3. Toggle EPOS Now Payments on. Toggle Merchant Copy and Customer Copy off if extra receipts are not needed.

Cellular Data

  1. Go to Settings → Network & Internet → Cellular Data.
  2. Enter access code 0779.
  3. Confirm Cellular Data is Active, turn Wi-Fi off, and confirm 4G appears.
  4. Swipe up and select the Home button.

45-Day Activation & 28-Day Minimum Processing Requirements

Clause 10.4 — Activation and Minimum Transaction Requirements

Initial activation

Activate the POS Terminal within 45 days of entering into the Agreement and process at least 50 transactions within that period.

Ongoing requirements

During each rolling 28-day period for the duration of the Initial Term, meet at least one requirement:

  • Process at least 75% of your card processing; or
  • Process card payments totaling at least 2,000 in the agreement's applicable currency.

Inactivity fee

If the minimum processing level is not met for the duration of the Initial Term, EPOS Now may, by written notice, apply an inactivity fee of 49.00 per month per POS Terminal.

If the minimum is met for a subsequent 28-day period, the fee is suspended for as long as the threshold continues to be met. Exceptions may apply under the agreement.

Prohibited & Restricted Products and Services

The supplied policy describes products and services prohibited or restricted when using EPOS Now Payments (ENPS). This is a high-level index, not the complete policy.

Prohibited categories include

  • Illegal drugs and drug-production products.
  • Pyramid selling, certain loan activities, and timeshares.
  • Adult entertainment and sexual services.
  • Counterfeit goods and intellectual-property infringement.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives, signal jammers, and spy devices/software.
  • Illegal, harmful, deceptive, or certain unlicensed gambling activities.
  • Specified deceptive telemarketing and marketing practices.

Restricted categories

The supplied text identifies certain gambling, financial and money-service businesses, charities, investment activity, private medical practices, car sales, multi-level marketing, and other categories as requiring approval in specified circumstances.

E-commerce / card-not-present

The supplied text also lists travel, ticket agencies, tobacco and alcohol, prescription medicines, certain supplements making health claims, cloud storage/file-sharing, and pseudoscience-related services as requiring prior written permission for e-commerce/card-not-present transactions.

Duration, Termination & Cancellation

Duration

The supplied terms say the agreement runs for 12, 24, or 36 months, as specified in the Tailored Proposal or invoice. After the fixed period, it continues month-to-month until either party gives at least two months' written notice.

Termination

The supplied terms state that the customer may terminate immediately if service availability in a calendar month is below 90%. They also describe circumstances in which EPOS Now may terminate or suspend services, including suspected legal or contractual breaches, suspicious transactions, changes without written permission, or requirements from relevant banking/card-scheme parties.
